# Now you: design your own agentic workflow

**In pairs · ~20 minutes · paper and pen**

1. Pick a **recurring task** from your company (look things up in 2–3 systems, produce a draft)
2. Fill in the **worksheet**: the idea, example requests, why it needs an agent
3. **Find candidate MCP servers** in the directories *(QR codes on the worksheet — or use the printed server menu)*
4. Run each server through the **vetting checklist** → verdict 🟢🟡🔴
5. Note your **next steps to a pilot**

**Afterwards: 2-minute pitches — your idea, your servers, your verdict.**
